Trash first

What it is, why it’s safe, whether it regenerates, plus a risk level: safe, review or caution.

Scan. Review. Reclaim.

Three steps, and you stay in control of each one.

Scan

Smart Scan runs every enabled rule, streams progress and can be cancelled at any time.

Review

Safe items are pre-selected; Review and Caution items are not. Check the list and the total.

Reclaim

Items move to the Trash. Each is re-checked just before, and skipped if it changed since the scan.

More than a cleaner

One app instead of five separate tools.

Disk map

Sunburst drill-down and a large & old files finder.

Uninstaller & leftovers

Remove apps with their support files. Leftovers favour precision: a lone settings file is never offered.

Project cleanup

Skips projects active in the last 14 days and anything git tracks.

Exact duplicates

Hash-verified loose user files only. The kept copy must still match at clean time.

Menu bar & schedules

Free space at a glance and low-disk alerts. Notify only: Fallow never cleans on its own.

Honest about macOS

Purgeable space and APFS snapshots are shown as information, never claimed as freed.

The rules it can’t break

Enforced in code and covered by tests on temporary fixture folders.

  1. Trash by default. Permanent delete only behind a setting and a second confirmation.
  2. No removal without Review. Every item and the total are listed first.
  3. Re-check before deleting. Changed since the scan means skipped.
  4. Tool-owned data goes through its tool. Simulators use xcrun simctl.
  5. No volume crossing unless you picked the volume.
